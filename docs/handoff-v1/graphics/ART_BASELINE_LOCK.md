## Canonical playable trio — immutable order

1. **Ashen Monk** — Ember / Moss. Burns Vigil for power; big single hits. Prayer Beads: first Tap each turn pays +2 Ink.
2. **Quarry Witch** — Tide / Violet. Repaints the board and builds Spectrum Runs. Chisel Wand: once per fight, recolor one block free.
3. **The Unlit** — Gold / Ash. Turns enemy Ash into fuel. Hollow Crown: each Ash block popped pays 1 Spark.

Ashen Monk is mandatory in class select and remains first. The prior painterly Monk portrait is preserved. Quarry Witch remains Tide/Violet. The Unlit's v0.4 painterly pale/silver-haired Gold/Ash portrait supersedes the earlier vector concept; the vector is archived as fallback.

**Shade is not a playable class.** It is the previous dead deck represented as an encounter. **Act I boss is The Hollow Warden**, not a player Warden class.

## Visual material language

Torchlit Cairn, gothic crypt, stained-glass rose, carved runes, braziers, memory fragments, worn slate, blackened iron, aged vellum, restrained antique gold, readable bone text. The five jewel hues are Ember, Tide, Moss, Gold and Violet. Ash, Stone and Prism remain distinct neutral block types. Every hue has glyph + color.

## Anti-drift

Do not introduce Vanguard, Arcanist, Warden, Bladebound, Bladeborn, Veilcaller, Inkweaver or Shade as replacement playable classes. Do not turn the board into swap-match-3 or falling blocks, do not switch to landscape, do not use a flattened artboard as interactive UI, and do not replace the visual baseline with unrelated generated artboards.
